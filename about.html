Living most of my life in Wisconsin has given me a near-constant desire for good cheese. I grew up in a small town just north of La Crosse, straddling the divide between rural and suburban, and after high school, I was lucky enough to attend UW–Madison. In Madison I found wisdom and knowledge I never expected, friends to last a lifetime, and a community that welcomed me for the next ten years of my life.

After working in Wisconsin's libraryland for several years, I'm now living in Minneapolis with my two cats and working on my MGIS degree. My hope is to work in the public sector and help improve how we communicate spatial information with the public. In short, I want to help people connect more with the world around them.
